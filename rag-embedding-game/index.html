    What you're actually looking at

    Every word here is a vector — a list of 16 numbers describing how aquatic, technological, emotional, cosmic… it is. That is an embedding: meaning turned into geometry.

    • Similarity is distance. Words that mean similar things point the same way (high cosine similarity). Hover any dot to see its top axes.
    • Retrieval = nearest neighbours. A RAG system embeds your question, then grabs the closest chunks. That's exactly what Probe does.
    • The board is a projection. You can't see 16 dimensions, so we run PCA to flatten it to 2-D — the same trick used to visualize real embeddings.
    • A “tile” is a region, not a square. Each shaded blob is a cluster of related meaning. A bridge word lives between two regions because it scores high on both — that's how RAG stitches distant context together.

    The three ways to play

    Solo · Probe & Bridge — connect all eight regions into one graph by finding words that straddle two of them. Fewer guesses & stronger bridges = better score.

    1v1 · Encirclement — Go, played on the concept graph. Claim a region by naming a word that lands inside it; build bridges (walls) between regions you own; surround an opponent group on every side to capture it.

    Teams · Structures — several players per side take turns building one linked conceptual structure, scored by the largest connected territory that encircles the other team.
